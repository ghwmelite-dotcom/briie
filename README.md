> *Briie just started as a counselor at Brilla Prep. On Day 1, she proposed something wild: a token that actually helps students.*

**$BRIIE** is the official community token of [Brilla Prep](https://brillaprep.org). Hold $BRIIE, save on prep courses, earn while you study, and help fund scholarships for students who need them.

---

## The Flywheel

```
More Students → More Revenue → Token Buybacks/Burns → Higher $BRIIE Value
     ↑                                                      ↓
Token Discounts ← Students Buy/Hold $BRIIE ← Students Save on Prep ←
```

---

## Utility

| Tier | $BRIIE Held | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Student** | Any amount | 5% off all Brilla Prep courses |
| **Scholar** | 50K+ | 15% off + early access to new tests |
| **Dean's List** | 250K+ | 25% off + Briie office hours |
| **Valedictorian** | 1M+ | Free unlimited prep + name a module |

### Study-to-Earn
Complete a full Brilla Prep practice test → earn $BRIIE.

### Scholarship Fund
1% of every transaction feeds a scholarship pool for students who can't afford prep.

## Platform Coupling

| Brilla Prep Milestone | $BRIIE Action |
|---|---|
| 100 signups | Burn 1% of supply |
| First paying student | $500 market buy + airdrop |
| 500 tests completed | Study-to-earn airdrop |
| Monthly revenue target | 5% of revenue → buyback |
